How to Play

  • 🧾 Grab a ticket from the counter.
  • 🧑‍🍳 Each player runs two random stations.
  • 🤝 Need another station? Use the dual-touch airlock.
  • 🚨 Drinks only move when both sides hold the airlock.
  • 🏆 Drop finished drinks on their ticket to score.
